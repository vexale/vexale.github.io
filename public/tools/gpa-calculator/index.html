GPA calculator: understand the weighted formula

A grade point average combines course grades with the number of credits each course carries. Use the formula and worked example below, then check your institution’s grading scale before relying on a result.

Weighted GPA formula

GPA = total quality points ÷ total credit hours

For each course, multiply its grade-point value by its credit hours. Add those quality points across your courses, then divide by the total credits included in the calculation.

Worked example

Divide 29.9 quality points by 9 credits: the example GPA is about 3.32 when rounded to two decimal places.

How to calculate your GPA

  1. Find the grade-point value assigned to each course on your school’s official scale.
  2. Multiply each value by that course’s credit hours.
  3. Add the quality points and the included credit hours separately.
  4. Divide the quality-point total by the credit-hour total; follow your institution’s rounding and repeat-course rules.

Grade values, weighted courses, pass/fail classes, and rounding rules vary by institution. This example explains the method and is not an official transcript calculation.
