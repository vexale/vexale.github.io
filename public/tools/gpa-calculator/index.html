Weighted average: understand the formula

A weighted average combines values with their assigned weights. Use the formula and worked example below, then confirm that the chosen weights fit your needs.

Weighted average formula

Weighted average = sum of (value × weight) ÷ sum of weights

For each item, multiply its value by its weight. Add those weighted values, then divide by the sum of the weights included in the calculation.

How to calculate a weighted average

  1. Choose the value for each item using the scale that fits your purpose.
  2. Multiply each value by its assigned weight.
  3. Add the weighted values and the included weights separately.
  4. Divide the weighted-value total by the total weight, then round the result as needed.

Values, weights, and rounding rules depend on your use case. This example explains the method and is not an official assessment.
