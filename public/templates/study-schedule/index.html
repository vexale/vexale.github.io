Make the schedule work for you

  1. Add fixed appointments, work, meals, and travel first.
  2. Schedule demanding tasks when your energy is usually highest.
  3. Break large projects into smaller tasks and leave time to check progress.
  4. Leave recovery time and a buffer for tasks that take longer than expected.
  5. Review the plan weekly and move unfinished work deliberately instead of filling every open hour.

This is a flexible example, not a required timetable. Adjust it for your workload, time zone, responsibilities, and rest needs.
