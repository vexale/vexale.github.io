Make the schedule work for you

  1. Add fixed classes, work, meals, and travel first.
  2. Place demanding study blocks when your energy is usually highest.
  3. Break large assignments into smaller sessions and add short review blocks.
  4. Leave recovery time and a buffer for tasks that take longer than expected.
  5. Review the plan weekly and move unfinished work deliberately instead of filling every open hour.

This is a flexible example, not a required timetable. Adjust it for your course load, time zone, responsibilities, and rest needs.
